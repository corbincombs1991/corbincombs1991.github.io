OUR STORY / THE WAY WE SEE IT

Some things
stay with you.

A song on a mixtape. The jacket you borrowed and never gave back. The jeans that feel like they were made for you.

THAT'S THE AFTERGLOW.

Clothes with a past.
Style in the present.

Afterglow Vintage is a love letter to getting dressed your own way. To faded colors, unexpected combinations, and pieces that don’t need to be perfect to be your favorite.

Our inspiration comes from the 90s: the ease, the edge, the feeling that you could make something entirely yours. But this isn’t a costume. It’s a starting point.

Wear it loose. Mix the decades. Make a little history.
