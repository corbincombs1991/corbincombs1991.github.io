A LITTLE GOOD TO KNOW

Good questions.
Useful answers.

How do I buy a piece?+

Available pieces are sold through Depop. Open the shop profile, read the individual listing, and complete your purchase there. This site does not collect payments.

How do I know it will fit?+

Vintage size labels can differ from current sizing. Compare the measurements in the listing with a similar garment you already own. If measurements are missing, ask the seller before purchasing.

What condition should I expect?+

Previously worn clothing can show signs of wear. Use the photos and condition notes in each listing to decide whether a piece is right for you. Ask for a closer look at any detail you are unsure about.

What about shipping and returns?+

Shipping costs, destinations, dispatch details, and any seller return terms are shown on Depop. Check the current listing and Depop policies before buying; this website does not set separate terms.

How do I care for vintage clothing?+

Start with the garment’s care label. If it is missing or the fabric is delicate, ask a professional cleaner. Air pieces between wears, store them away from direct sunlight, and treat older seams and trims gently.

Are the editorial photos items for sale?+

The editorial and lookbook images are created to illustrate the Afterglow style. They do not represent available inventory. Product photos, prices, and stock are provided in the actual Depop listings.
