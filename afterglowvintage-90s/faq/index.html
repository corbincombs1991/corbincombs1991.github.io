How do I buy a piece?+

Click a product or visit our Depop profile. Read the listing and complete your purchase on Depop. This website does not take payments.

How do I know it will fit?+

Vintage sizing can differ from modern sizing. Check the listing’s measurements against a piece you already own, and ask the seller on Depop if you need more details.

What condition should I expect?+

Vintage clothes have a history. Review the product photos and condition notes on each Depop listing before you buy.

What about shipping and returns?+

Check the seller’s current information and the terms shown on Depop before purchasing. Shipping, returns, and buyer support are handled there.

How do I care for vintage clothing?+

Start with the garment’s care label. Use gentle washing and air drying when appropriate, and seek specialist advice for delicate fabrics or leather.

Is the guestbook public?+

No. Guestbook notes are saved only in your current browser, on your device. They are not sent to Afterglow or shared with other visitors.

Where’s that music coming from?+

The optional Afterglow Radio makes an original little tune in your browser. It only plays after you press Play. Press Stop any time.

Are the lookbook photos products for sale?+

The lookbook uses original generated editorial art for styling inspiration. Product cards show the real Depop listings.
