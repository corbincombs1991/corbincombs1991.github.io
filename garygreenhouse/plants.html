Our plants

Good things grow here.

Find something for your porch, something for your garden, and something you didn’t know you needed. Explore the plants and combinations we’re known for.

Plant collections

A greenhouse favorite

Hanging baskets

A little color at every level.

Gary Greenhouse has raised more than 10,000 hanging baskets, with impatiens, begonias, geraniums, petunias, tropical plants, foliage, and hundreds of combinations. The hanging-basket collection has filled more than 15 greenhouses.

Our signature showstoppers

Giant moss baskets

Generous blooms. A statement all their own.

Moss combination baskets in 14, 18, 20, and 24-inch sizes. Look for the spectacular 24-inch Wave petunia and 20-inch Proven Winners baskets highlighted in our collection.

Made for sun & shade

Combination planters

Find a mix that feels like you.

Hundreds of Proven Winners combinations in containers and hanging baskets, with selections for both sunny spots and shady corners.

Easygoing summer color

Wave petunias

From a window box to a garden bed.

A full line of Wave petunias in cell packs, individual pots, hanging baskets, and six-packs. Explore the colorful annuals and perennials that fill the greenhouse in spring.

The beginning of spring

Pansies & violas

Small flowers. A very welcome sight.

A wide range of colors for the cooler growing season. Our early pansies and violas are grown for cold tolerance and traditionally become available in early March. Ask us about weather and planting conditions.

Autumn favorites

Garden mums & asters

A porch full of fall.

Our growing collection has included more than 15,000 hardy Yoder and Igloo garden mums, along with fall asters. Offered in pots, hanging baskets, and 12-inch tubs. Call to confirm seasonal availability.

Vegetable plants are part of our roots, too. Start a kitchen garden, fill a raised bed, or find a new favorite for your summer harvest.

Ask about vegetable plants ↗

Tomatoes, every which way.

Our selection has included more than 50 tomato varieties, from old heirlooms to newer F1 hybrids. Choose from cell packs, single plants, and flats. Larger staked tomatoes may even have fruit already growing.

Looking for grafted tomatoes, including Mighty ’Mato varieties? Please call about current availability.
