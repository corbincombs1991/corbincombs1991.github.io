From the greenhouse archive

The original site preserves past community events, including the 2010 Indianapolis Home and Flower Show at Lucas Oil Stadium (March 5–7), the 2013 IPGA Giant Pumpkin Show (October 5), and the 2015 Spring Open House (April 25–26). The 2015 open house featured hot dogs and soda from 10 am–4 pm and a $50 gift-certificate drawing. These are past events; old dates, giveaways, ticket offers, and calendar hours are not current announcements.
