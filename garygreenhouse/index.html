Our roots run deep

Family grown.
Indianapolis loved.

For more than 70 years, Gary Greenhouse has been part of the south side’s growing season. We’re a third-generation family business with five acres of greenhouses and nursery space on Bluff Road.

Our specialties are the things that make a garden feel like yours: generous hanging baskets, unique combination planters, colorful bedding plants, vegetables, perennials, shrubs, and bushes.
